# lndcai001

Project code for my KAIST GSAI application. Not a brand. A working folder.

---

## what this is

I am applying to KAIST GSAI for Spring 2027. The portal closes 1 September. This folder holds the materials I am building for that application.

Three things are here:

1. **Literature review.** Notes from papers I have read. Structured by topic, not dumped.
2. **Diagrams.** Architecture sketches and flow diagrams that show how I think about the work.
3. **Progress log.** What I have done, what is left, and where I am stuck.

---

## milestones

The application has three concrete checkpoints:

- **Personal statement.** Drafted. Needs one more revision pass before I ship it.
- **Project description.** Outlined. The diagrams in this folder feed into it.
- **Reference letters.** Two requested. One confirmed. One pending.

I am targeting a complete application bundle by 28 August. The portal deadline is 1 September. That gap is buffer, not slack.

## what i am using this for

The GSAI program at KAIST starts Spring 2027. I am applying to work with specific faculty on specific problems. This folder is the evidence base for that application.

The work here is real. I did it. I am not padding this with hypotheticals or third-hand summaries. When I cite a paper, I read it. When I draw a diagram, I built the system it describes or I am building it.
